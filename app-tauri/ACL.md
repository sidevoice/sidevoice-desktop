# Who may call what: the plugin's commands and their authorization

`sidevoice-app-tauri`'s commands, one row each. The page invokes `plugin:sidevoice-app-tauri|<command>`; an app grants it
to a window with the permission in the second column (`allow-<command>` is written for each command by the plugin's build
script, `permissions/autogenerated/`). `cargo test` keeps this table, `commands.rs` and the permission files the same
list; the app's own tests keep its capabilities to it (the room window is granted all of them, the call controls
card's window none, no other window any).

- **Window.** Where the app grants it: `room` is the app's room window, the page that owns the calls.
- **Caller.** What the command asks of who invoked it before it does anything. `room page`: the app's own answer to
  whether the webview is the page it owns the calls for (the desktop: the current room window, showing its own origin);
  refused as `bad_request`. `capability`: nothing beyond the window the permission was granted to.
- **Arguments.** What the page sends, as it names them (`snake_case` in Rust, `camelCase` on the wire).
- **Tells the page.** What the command makes the app's `EventSink` hand to the page, besides its answer. The events are
  evaluated in the room page as `window.__sidevoiceDesktop.voiceEvent(event)`, `{key, type, data?}`; the call's are
  `turn`, `state`, `level`, `playback-level`, `error` and `take`, the host's `say` and `devices`.
- **Card.** Whether the call controls card's window may invoke it: never.
